Many small-scale detergent producers and
academic laboratories lack access to affordable,
properly designed batch reactors, leading to
poor mixing,safety risks,and inconsistent detergent quality.
There is therefore a need to design and fabricate a low-cost,
laboratory-scale batch reactor that can efficiently produce uniformly mixed powdered detergent.
